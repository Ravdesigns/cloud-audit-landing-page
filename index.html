Good questions.
Straight answers.

What’s included for free?

Your 20 largest compute and block-storage savings, with resource names, usage evidence and related security or compliance flags. You also receive aggregate counts and estimated costs for idle and orphaned resources.

When will I get my results?

Within 24 hours after the required read-only access is ready. Sending this form requests the setup instructions; it does not start the audit clock. We’ll flag any missing access or metric history before analysis.

Can the audit change anything in production?

No. The audit uses read-only access to inspect resource configuration and usage. It does not deploy, resize, stop or delete resources. Your team reviews the permissions and can revoke access.

What does the optional full report add?

The $5,000 one-time report names the additional findings, including idle and orphaned resources, and extends recommendations to databases, networking, functions, storage and caches. It includes remediation steps, engineer review and one re-run at six months. Delivery is five business days, with scope and fee confirmed before the paid engagement. There is no subscription or automatic upgrade.

Are the savings guaranteed?

No. Savings are estimates based on your resource configuration, usage and pricing. Your team should validate workload needs before making changes. The example report above demonstrates the format and is not a customer result.
